1. Paris, France

Paris is the capital city of France. It is famous for its beautiful architecture, romantic atmosphere, art, fashion and culture. It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in the world.

Famous Attractions

  1. Eiffel Tower
  2. Louvre Museum
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral
  4. Arc de Triomphe

Explore more: Official Paris Tourism Website

2. Switzerland

Switzerland is a beautiful country known for its snow-covered mountains, peaceful lakes, green landscapes and scenic railway journeys. It is a perfect destination for nature lovers.

Famous Attractions

  1. Mount Titlis
  2. Jungfraujoch
  3. Lake Geneva
  4. Interlaken

Explore more: Official Switzerland Tourism Website

3. Goa, India

Goa is a popular tourist destination in India. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, Portuguese architecture, seafood and exciting water activities.

Famous Attractions

  1. Baga Beach
  2. Calangute Beach
  3. Fort Aguada
  4. Basilica of Bom Jesus

Explore more: Official Goa Tourism Website

4. Dubai, UAE

Dubai is a modern city in the United Arab Emirates. It is famous for its luxurious shopping malls, impressive skyscrapers, desert safaris and modern architecture.

Famous Attractions

  1. Burj Khalifa
  2. Dubai Mall
  3. Palm Jumeirah
  4. Dubai Fountain

Explore more: Official Dubai Tourism Website

5. Maldives

The Maldives is a tropical island destination famous for its clear blue water, white sandy beaches, beautiful resorts and underwater marine life. It is an ideal destination for relaxation.

Famous Attractions

  1. Male City
  2. Maafushi Island
  3. Banana Reef
  4. Vaadhoo Island

Explore more: Official Maldives Tourism Website
